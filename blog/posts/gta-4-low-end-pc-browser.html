Separate network and device limits

A locally rendered game depends on device hardware, while a streamed game depends more on latency, network stability, and video decoding.

Improve connection stability

Pause large downloads, move closer to the router, and test at a similar time of day. A high speed-test result does not guarantee low input delay.

Know service-side limits

A distant server or overloaded provider can add delay beyond your control. Compare results before changing unrelated settings.
