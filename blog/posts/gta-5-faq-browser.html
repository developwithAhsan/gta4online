Does every browser page run the full PC game?

No. Some pages link to streaming services or embed third-party players. Confirm how the service works.

Is no-download access always free?

No. Providers may require an account, queue, subscription, or other conditions. Read terms before paying.

What if the player does not load?

Check the network, reload once, and review the provider help page. The service may be offline or block embedding.
