PRIVACY AND DATA

Privacy notice.

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This notice explains the information that may be processed when you visit GTA 4 Online Web Edition and the small preferences the site stores in your browser. This version is an independent static information portal; it does not offer visitor accounts, payment checkout, or a connected gameplay service.

1. Information used to deliver the website

When a browser requests a page, image, stylesheet, or script, the hosting and content-delivery providers may process technical request information to route, secure, and troubleshoot that request. Depending on provider configuration, this may include an IP address, requested URL, timestamp, browser or device details, referrer information, and security diagnostics. The hosting provider's own privacy terms apply to its infrastructure processing.

2. Browser-side preferences

The touch-control interface stores display preferences in local storage under gta4-touch-settings. The language selector stores the selected site language under gta4-site-language. These values remember the interface layout and language in the same browser. They are not GTA IV gameplay saves, account credentials, or mission-progress data.

You can remove these values by clearing this website's stored data in your browser. Doing so may reset the touch layout and language selection. Local storage is a browser storage feature and is distinct from an HTTP cookie.

3. External resources and links

The website requests font files from Google Fonts. When your browser contacts an external resource provider, that provider may receive technical request information such as an IP address, browser details, and requested resource. Links to GitHub issue tracking and other external websites take you to separate services with their own privacy notices and terms.

A separate cloud gaming provider may process account, payment, device, session, and gameplay information. This portal does not operate such a service and does not control its data practices. Review the provider's privacy notice before creating an account or paying.

4. Forms, accounts, and gameplay data

This version does not provide visitor registration, user profiles, a first-party contact form, payment processing, or a compatible GTA IV gameplay client. It therefore does not store a visitor's GTA IV mission progress or game account. Website issue reports are handled through GitHub, a separate third-party service.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

The site code uses browser local storage for the preferences described above. Hosting infrastructure and external services may process technical data or use their own technologies under their configurations and policies. If analytics, advertising, embedded comments, or a gameplay provider is introduced later, the notice should be reviewed and updated to describe that service before it is used.

6. Children's privacy and sensitive information

The site is a general game information resource and does not intentionally ask visitors to submit sensitive personal information. Do not submit passwords, recovery codes, payment details, or identity documents through a public issue tracker. Parents and guardians should review the terms and privacy settings of external gaming services used by children.

7. Security, retention, and changes

We aim to limit the information this website asks visitors to provide. Technical request logs and diagnostics are controlled in part by the hosting provider and are retained under that provider's policies. Browser-side preferences remain on the device until removed by the visitor or browser.

This notice may change when the website's functionality or service providers change. It is general website information, not legal advice and not a substitute for the privacy terms of external providers.

Contact about privacy

For a website privacy concern, use the Contact page. Keep private credentials and personal documents out of public reports.
