CONTACT THE PROJECT

Website feedback and issue reports.

Clear, reproducible feedback helps us improve the GTA 4 Online Web Edition website, its guides, and its accessibility. This page explains the available reporting route and what information makes a report useful.

Where to send a report

This static website does not currently provide a first-party contact form or a dedicated support email address. To report a website issue, use the project's public GitHub issue tracker:

Open the project issue tracker ↗

What to include

  • The exact page URL where the issue occurs.
  • Your device type and operating system.
  • The browser name and version, if known.
  • The steps that reproduce the issue and what you expected to happen.
  • The exact error message, if one is displayed.

Privacy and safety

The GitHub issue tracker is a public third-party service. Do not post passwords, browser cookies, session tokens, recovery codes, payment information, or personal documents. Review GitHub's own privacy and account settings before submitting a public report.

Game support is separate

GTA 4 Online Web Edition is an independent information portal. A compatible GTA IV gameplay client is not connected to this website. We cannot provide Rockstar account recovery, game activation, purchase, save-file, or third-party cloud-service support. For those matters, contact the publisher, platform, or provider through its official support channel.

Editorial corrections

For a factual correction, include the article URL, the claim you believe is inaccurate, and a reliable source that supports the correction. That gives us something concrete to review without guessing at the intended change.
