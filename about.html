GTA 5 Online Web Edition is a simple website that helps visitors open a third-party browser game player, with a clear Play Game launch screen, fullscreen option, quick controls, and troubleshooting information.

The Play Game button loads a separate third-party website inside an embedded frame. We do not control its content, availability, compatibility, or privacy practices. If the provider blocks embedding or is unavailable, use its direct link from the game-player fallback.
