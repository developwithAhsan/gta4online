ABOUT THE PROJECT

An independent GTA IV information portal.

Last updated: November 17, 2026

GTA 4 Online Web Edition is an independent fan information project. It brings Grand Theft Auto IV reference material and practical device guidance together in a responsive website for desktop, Chromebook, tablet, and mobile visitors.

Our purpose

The project helps visitors understand GTA IV PC controls, device compatibility, browser-game claims, fullscreen viewing, troubleshooting, privacy, and safe browsing. Our aim is to give people clear information before they install software, share account details, or pay for a third-party service.

What the website includes

  • A responsive information portal with GTA IV-themed navigation and page layouts.
  • A keyboard and mouse control reference for the PC release, alongside links to publisher documentation where relevant.
  • A mobile touch-control interface with adjustable size, opacity, sensitivity, and left-handed layout preferences.
  • A gallery of GTA IV gameplay screenshots and a searchable library of focused guides.
  • Editorial and policy pages covering project details, contact, privacy, cookies, terms, and disclaimers.

Current gameplay status

A compatible GTA IV gameplay client is not connected to this website. The portal does not contain the original GTA IV game engine, host or distribute original game files, stream the PC release, or operate a multiplayer backend. Its touch overlay is an interface feature; a compatible game client would need to receive and translate those inputs before they could control a game.

Editorial approach

Each guide is intended to answer a specific visitor question. We distinguish site features from third-party services, identify important limitations, and link to authoritative documentation where it helps. We do not treat a search phrase or page title as evidence that the site provides a feature it does not have.

Independence and trademarks

Grand Theft Auto, GTA IV, Liberty City, and related names and marks belong to their respective rights holders. This website is an independent fan project and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

Feedback

For website defects, accessibility problems, broken links, or factual corrections, use the Contact page. Include the page URL and reproducible details, and do not post passwords, session cookies, or private account information.
