COOKIE AND STORAGE INFORMATION

Cookies and local storage.

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This page explains the browser storage used by GTA 4 Online Web Edition. Cookies and local storage are different technologies: both can retain information in a browser, but they are not interchangeable.

What this website saves locally

The touch-control interface uses browser local storage under gta4-touch-settings to remember choices such as overlay size, opacity, sensitivity, and left-handed layout. The language selector uses gta4-site-language to remember the selected site language.

These settings are stored in the browser on your device. They do not contain GTA IV game progress, payment details, or a website account. Clearing this website's stored data may reset these preferences.

Cookies

The current site code does not use a first-party login or checkout system. This does not mean that every external service or the hosting provider is incapable of using cookies or similar technologies. Requests for fonts, external links, and infrastructure services are handled under the relevant provider's own policies.

How to manage stored data

Use your browser's site settings or privacy controls to inspect and remove data for this website. Menu wording varies between browsers and operating systems. Removing site data can reset the preferences described above and other browser state for the site.

Changes to this policy

If the project adds analytics, advertising, an embedded comment provider, a contact form, or a gameplay service, the storage and cookie information should be reviewed and updated to reflect that functionality. This page is a general explanation and is not legal advice.

More information

Read the Privacy Notice for information about website requests and external resources, or use the Contact page to report a website privacy concern.
